QUICK GUIDE · NO API KEY

Use Gemini for free

  1. Enter the product name and verified details, then add up to five product photos.
  2. Choose “Prepare 11 prompts,” then “Download for Gemini · one ZIP.”
  3. In the Gemini companion app, choose Manual Import → ZIP Package, upload that ZIP, and review the imported photos and prompts.
  4. Choose “Generate All 11 Images.” Gemini may apply its own free-use limits; review every result before listing.

Listing Studio does not call Gemini or use a paid API. The ZIP contains the photos you chose, so upload it only when you want to share those references with Gemini.
